title: Play by touch in the browser
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets: []
credit: [Gunnar Beutner]
---

A touch screen plays the browser build. One finger is the left button, whether tapped,
dragged, or held, which is the right button and so the game's cancel and deselect. Two
fingers dragged pan the map, and a two finger tap is a second way to reach the right
button. A finger resting on a button or menu item presses it and lifting triggers it, a
finger leaves no pointer behind, so a structure is placed with two taps, and a trackpad
scroll pans the battlefield while a wheel scrolls the sidebar. [Touch
controls](/systems/touch-controls/) owns every gesture. Only the tap worked before. Mouse and
keyboard play is unchanged, and a device with both answers to either.
